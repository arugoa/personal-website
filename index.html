01 — About

Who I am

I'm Arihant Jain, a computer engineer finishing my B.S. at UC San Diego (GPA 3.78, major GPA 3.96) and continuing into a master's in ML/DS focused on robotic systems and control. My work sits where perception, estimation, and decision-making meet — I like taking raw sensor streams and turning them into something a robot can actually act on.

Most of my time goes to state estimation and autonomy: filtering and optimization for SLAM, sampling- and search-based planning, and optimal control for real platforms. At the Existential Robotics Lab I build latent world-dynamics models for safety filtering; at Triton Robotics I lead the embedded stack, from motor tuning to LiDAR-based autonomy.

The projects below span two graduate robotics sequences — sensing & estimation and planning & optimal control — plus research and competition work. Each links to a full write-up.
